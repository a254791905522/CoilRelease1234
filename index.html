CoilRelease is a bright factory puzzle. Each level is a riveted metal chamber. Tap a colored node on the rim, send a golden spring racing along the inside, and lock it onto the blue flag. The path is short. The margin for error is shorter.

How a chamber works

Four beats. No timer shouting at you — just the shape of the metal and the spring you choose to let go.

1

Read the pocket

Look at the riveted outline, the blue flags, and the colored nodes parked on the rim. The coil only travels the interior.

2

Pick a node

Tap a release node. The golden spring unwinds from that point and traces a path toward a flag.

3

Stop on the flag

The spring should lock onto the blue target. Too long, or the wrong bend, and the shot is an Overshoot.

4

Bank the keys

Clean sequencing fills the key row. Spend the three lives wisely — a drained meter restarts the whole level.

Overshoot is the whole lesson

Power is easy. Stopping is the puzzle. If the coil runs past the flag or takes a bend that never meets it, you lose a life and the spring snaps back. The factory does not give partial credit for almost.

Pipes change the math

Deeper stages split the machine into stacked chambers. Valves and pipes tie them together, so you plan several coils and several flags in one sequence. The goal stays the same: three golden keys, machine still running.
